    Azeroth Pinpoint

    Unfurling the map of Azeroth…

    How to play

    Every day, the Cartographers' Guild names five places in Azeroth. Find them.

    1. 1
      Read the clue. A town, dungeon entrance, capital or a whole zone.
    2. 2
      Drop your pin. Click or tap the map – drag the pin to fine-tune. Zoom in and the parchment gives way to the land itself.
    3. 3
      Confirm (or press Enter) to reveal the answer. Each pin scores up to 100 for accuracy – for zones and capitals, anywhere inside is a bullseye.
    4. 4
      The stakes rise. Locations 1–2 are worth 100, location 3 is ×2 and locations 4–5 are ×3 – a perfect expedition scores 1,000.
    5. 5
      Stuck? The hint reveals the continent, at the cost of 25% of that round.
    🎯 95+🟩 75+🟨 50+🟧 25+🟥 under 25

    A new expedition unlocks every midnight (your local time). Same five places for everyone.
